About

Risk, measured and closed.

Security analyst trained at the University of Maryland, now based in Pune and looking for my next vulnerability management or SOC team.

Most of my work sits between the scanner and the fix. I run Qualys VMDR and Nessus at scale, sort real risk from noise, route each finding to the team that owns it, and keep chasing until it's closed inside SLA.

At ANGARAI I did this on-site in Maryland for public-sector clients, including the Comptroller of Maryland's FMIS modernization, where I audited every Maryland state agency. Around that I investigated SIEM alerts in Splunk, wrote compliance evidence for ISO 27001 and CIS Controls, and automated the repetitive parts with n8n and Python.
